Features

Nimodio covers the whole path from a long gaming recording to a published short video.

Find the highlights

  • Scans the recording folders you choose and detects new recordings automatically.
  • Finds intense moments from sound and motion and cuts them with the right lead-in and follow-up.
  • Swipe through suggestions: keep, discard or rate. Nimodio learns from your choices per game.
  • Works with long sessions and short replay clips.

Edit in a vertical timeline

  • Format: vertical full screen, or landscape with blurred or black background.
  • Trim, split, join, copy and paste clips; undo and redo.
  • Speed from 25 % to 3×, freeze frames, brightness, sharpness and zoom.
  • Crop to a region and let the frame follow a moving object.
  • Picture-in-picture, for example to show the kill feed.
  • Sound effects, music and stickers with volume lines directly on the waveform.
  • Hook text in the first seconds with adjustable length.
  • Subtitles recognised locally on your PC.

Look like your channel

  • Channel logo, colours and fonts on every video.
  • Intros and outros for every season, optional sponsor slots.
  • Designed cover images and profile pictures.

Plan and publish

  • Calendar with your posting days and times; distribute ready videos with one click.
  • Captions and hashtags per platform.
  • TikTok: send as draft to your TikTok inbox, or post directly with the privacy level, comments, Duet and Stitch settings you choose and your consent before each post.
  • YouTube: upload as Short with title, description, tags and privacy setting.
  • Instagram: publish Reels with caption and custom cover.
  • Or post yourself: copy the prepared caption and upload the video file in the app.

Learn what works

  • Views, likes, comments, shares and followers of your own posts over time.
  • Comparison with the previous period and a ranking of your best videos.

Edit on your phone

  • Open Nimodio on your phone in your home Wi-Fi with a QR code and PIN.
  • The same editor, built for touch.
