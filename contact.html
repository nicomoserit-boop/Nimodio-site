About & contact

Nimodio is developed and operated by Nico Moser, the creator behind the gaming channel Kelbsquad. It started as the tool we use ourselves to turn hours of gameplay into daily short videos.

Get Nimodio

Nimodio is a free desktop app for Windows 10 and 11. To get the installer, write us a short email with the platforms you post on. We reply with the download link and help you with the setup.
