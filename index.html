Everything from recording to post

Automatic highlights

Nimodio analyses sound and motion in your recordings and suggests the strongest moments as short clips. Swipe to keep or discard – it learns what you like.

Vertical editor

Trim, split, join and reorder clips, change speed, add freeze frames, zoom, track a moving object, add sounds, music and effects – on your PC or your phone.

Captions, hook and cover

Get caption and hook suggestions in your channel’s style, a hook text that appears in the first seconds and a designed cover image with your logo.

Your brand

Channel logo, colours, intro and outro are added to every video, so your clips look consistent across platforms.

Planning

Distribute ready videos over your posting days and times in a calendar. You decide what goes out and when.

Statistics

See views, likes, comments, shares and follower growth of your own posts in one place, compared with the previous period.

How it works

  1. RecordPlay and record as usual with OBS, NVIDIA, Xbox Game Bar or AMD. Nimodio watches the folder you choose.
  2. ReviewNimodio suggests highlight clips. Keep the good ones with a swipe and fine-tune them in the editor.
  3. SchedulePick a date and time in the planner, check caption and cover.
  4. PublishAt the scheduled time Nimodio uploads the video to the accounts you connected – or you post it yourself with the prepared caption.

Your accounts, your decision

You connect TikTok, YouTube or Instagram by signing in on the platform’s own page. Nimodio never sees your passwords. It only publishes videos that you approve or schedule, with the caption, privacy level and interaction settings you choose. You can disconnect at any time.

Private by design

Nimodio runs on your own PC. Your recordings, videos and account tokens stay there. There is no Nimodio cloud account, no tracking and no advertising. Read our Privacy Policy.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • Gaming recordings as MP4, MKV, MOV or similar
  • Optional: a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram professional account for direct publishing
  • Optional: a phone in the same Wi-Fi network to edit on the go
