Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Nimodio (“Nimodio” or the “App”), a desktop application for Windows that turns gaming recordings into short videos and publishes them to the creator’s own social media accounts, and to this website (nicomoserit-boop.github.io/Nimodio-site).

Who “we”, “us” and “our” are: Nimodio is developed and operated by Nico Moser, Chäsimatt 12, 6343 Rotkreuz, Switzerland. In this Privacy Policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Nico Moser as the developer and operator of Nimodio. “You” and “your” refer to the person who installs and uses Nimodio or visits this website.

Contact for all privacy questions: kelbsquad@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • Nimodio runs on your own Windows PC. There is no Nimodio user account and no Nimodio cloud server that stores your videos or account data.
  • Your recordings, videos, captions, settings and the access tokens of the accounts you connect are stored only locally on your PC. We do not receive them.
  • Nimodio sends data only directly from your PC to the services you choose to use (for example TikTok, YouTube or Instagram) and only to carry out what you ask it to do.
  • We do not sell personal data, do not use it for advertising, do not build profiles and do not share it with data brokers.
  • You can disconnect accounts and delete all data at any time (see section 10).

2. How Nimodio works

Nimodio is installed on your Windows PC. It analyses video files in folders you select, cuts highlights into vertical videos, suggests titles, captions and cover images, and lets you review, edit and schedule the videos. When you connect a social media account and approve or schedule a video, Nimodio uploads that video from your PC to the connected platform using the platform’s official API.

Nimodio can optionally show its interface on your phone. This works only inside your own local network, is switched off by default and is protected by a PIN you choose. No data passes through our servers.

3. Information Nimodio processes

3.1 Content you choose

  • Video recordings in the folders you select, the videos Nimodio creates from them, cover images, titles, captions, hashtags and hook texts.
  • Your ratings and edits (for example which clips you keep), which Nimodio uses locally to suggest better highlights in the future.

3.2 Information from accounts you connect

When you connect a social media account, you sign in on the platform’s own page and grant the permissions shown there. Nimodio then receives and stores locally:

  • Access and refresh tokens issued by the platform, so that Nimodio can act on your behalf.
  • Basic profile information such as your account ID, username, display name and profile picture, so Nimodio can show which account is connected.
  • Statistics of your own posts and account (for example views, likes, comments, shares and follower count), so Nimodio can show you how your videos perform.

3.3 Settings stored on your PC

  • Channel name, logo text, colours, posting schedule, folder paths and app preferences.
  • Optional API keys you enter yourself (for example an Anthropic, Freesound or Pixabay key).

3.4 Website

This website is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. When you visit it, GitHub, Inc. processes technical data such as your IP address and browser information in server logs to deliver and secure the website (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). We do not receive these logs. The page callback.html only passes the sign-in code from Instagram back to Nimodio on your own PC and stores nothing.

3.5 Contacting us

If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer you.

4. TikTok

If you connect a TikTok account, Nimodio uses TikTok’s Login Kit and Content Posting API with the following permissions:

  • user.info.basic – your open ID, display name and avatar, to show which TikTok account is connected.
  • user.info.stats – follower, like and video counts of your account, to show your growth in Nimodio.
  • video.list – a list of your own public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts, to show the performance of videos you published.
  • video.upload and video.publish – to send a video you selected to your TikTok account, either as a draft in your TikTok inbox or as a post with the caption, privacy level and interaction settings (comments, Duet, Stitch) that you choose in Nimodio before posting.

Before each post, Nimodio retrieves your creator information from TikTok (for example which privacy levels are available to you and the maximum video length) and shows it to you. Nothing is posted to TikTok without your action: a video is only published after you approve it or schedule it for a specific time.

TikTok data is used only to provide these functions to you. It is stored only on your PC, is not transferred to us, is not sold, is not used for advertising and is not shared with anyone other than TikTok itself. TikTok’s own processing is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

5. YouTube and Google user data

If you connect a YouTube channel, Nimodio uses the YouTube API Services with permission to upload videos (youtube.upload), read your channel information (youtube.readonly) and read analytics of your own videos (yt-analytics.readonly). Nimodio uses this access only to upload the videos you approve with the title, description, tags and privacy setting you choose, to show which channel is connected and to show statistics of your own videos.

Nimodio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not transferred to third parties, not used for advertising, not sold and not used to train AI models. By using the YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google’s processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Instagram (Meta)

If you connect an Instagram professional account, Nimodio uses the Instagram API with the permissions instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish and instagram_business_manage_insights, to show which account is connected, to publish the Reels you approve and to show statistics of your own posts. To set a custom cover image, Nimodio may place the cover image temporarily at a random public web address that Instagram retrieves; it is deleted afterwards. Meta’s processing is described in the Instagram Privacy Policy.

7. Other services you can choose to use

  • Freesound and Pixabay: when you search for sounds or video effects online in the editor, only your search term (and your own API key, if required) is sent. No videos or account data are transferred.
  • Anthropic (Claude), optional: only if you enter your own Anthropic API key and press a button for an AI feature (for example caption suggestions), Nimodio sends the text needed for that request (such as the game name, existing caption or channel description) and, only if you enable it, a few still frames of the video. Nimodio works fully without this feature.

8. Purposes and legal bases

Personal data is processed only to provide the functions of Nimodio that you use: creating and editing videos, connecting your accounts, publishing videos you approve, and showing statistics of your own content. Where the EU/EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) applies, the legal bases are the performance of our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), your consent when you connect an account or enable an optional feature (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you can withdraw at any time), and our legitimate interest in operating and securing this website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

9. Sharing and international transfers

We do not share, sell or rent your personal data. Data leaves your PC only when Nimodio sends it, on your instruction, to the services described in sections 4 to 7. These providers may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own privacy policies and safeguards. We have no access to the data exchanged between your PC and these services.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Data stays on your PC until you delete it. Access tokens are deleted when you click “Disconnect” in Nimodio (Account → Accounts).
  • To remove everything, uninstall Nimodio and delete its data folder (shown in Nimodio under Account → Settings). Your original recordings are never changed or deleted by Nimodio.
  • You can also revoke Nimodio’s access directly at the platform: TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and Instagram (Settings → Apps and websites).
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer your request.

11. Security

Nimodio stores data in your user profile on your PC, connects to platforms only over encrypted HTTPS connections using their official sign-in (OAuth), never asks for or stores your platform passwords, and protects the optional phone view with a PIN. Please keep your PC and Windows account secure, because anyone with access to them can access the data stored there.

12. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you have the right to request access to, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, to data portability, to object to processing and to withdraw consent at any time. Because Nimodio keeps your data on your own PC, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App. For anything else, contact us at kelbsquad@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), or the authority in your EU/EEA country of residence.

13. Children

Nimodio is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for anyone below the minimum age required by the platforms they connect. We do not knowingly process personal data of children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Nimodio changes or when the law requires it. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced on this website.
