Frequently asked questions

What is Nimodio?

Nimodio is a desktop app for Windows that turns your gaming recordings into short vertical videos and helps you publish them on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

Does Nimodio upload my recordings to a server?

No. Nimodio runs on your PC. Your recordings and videos stay there. A video only leaves your PC when Nimodio uploads it, on your instruction, to an account you connected. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I have to connect my social media accounts?

No. You can use Nimodio to find highlights, edit and plan videos and then upload them yourself with the prepared caption. Connecting accounts is optional and only needed for automatic publishing.

Will Nimodio post anything without my permission?

No. Nimodio only publishes videos that you approve or schedule. On TikTok you choose the privacy level and interaction settings before each post, or you send the video as a draft and finish it in the TikTok app.

Which data does Nimodio read from TikTok?

Your basic profile (display name and avatar), your account statistics, a list of your own videos with their statistics, and the creator information TikTok requires before posting. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I disconnect an account or delete my data?

In Nimodio go to Account → Accounts and click “Disconnect”. To remove everything, uninstall Nimodio and delete its data folder. You can also revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Which recording software works?

Any software that saves video files, for example OBS Studio, NVIDIA ShadowPlay, Xbox Game Bar or AMD ReLive.

Does Nimodio change or delete my original recordings?

No. Nimodio only reads your recordings and saves the finished videos in its own output folder.

What does Nimodio cost?

Nimodio is currently free. Optional AI caption suggestions use your own Anthropic API key, which is billed by Anthropic.

Is Nimodio affiliated with TikTok, YouTube or Instagram?

No. Nimodio is an independent app that uses the official APIs of these platforms.
